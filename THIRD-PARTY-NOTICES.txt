The globe imagery, coastlines and borders, plate boundaries and cloud imagery below are embedded in the app from GlobeCore
(external/GlobeCore). GlobeCore's own notices for them are in external/GlobeCore/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt; they are repeated in
full below, so this file alone carries every notice the app needs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Plate boundaries: PB2002
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Peter Bird (2003), An updated digital model of plate boundaries, Geochemistry,
Geophysics, Geosystems 4(3):1027 (PB2002). GIS conversion by Hugo Ahlenius, Nordpil
(https://github.com/fraxen/tectonicplates).

Licensed under the Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/
You are free to copy, distribute and use the data, to produce works from it and to
modify, transform and build upon it, as long as you attribute it as above. The steps were
merged into polylines by kind and simplified to 0.01 degrees (by EarthWatch3D's
tools/prepare-assets.ps1, which writes the result into GlobeCore's Earth pack).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Globe imagery: NASA Blue Marble
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation with Topography and Bathymetry
(July 2004, R. Stockli), resized to 8192x4096. NASA imagery is generally not
copyrighted (NASA Media Usage Guidelines).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Coastlines and borders: Natural Earth
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), 1:50m coastline and land boundary
lines. Public domain.

Natural Earth 5.1.2 admin-0 countries (1:50m), used by GlobeCore.Earth.CountryIndex. Public domain.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector (tag v5.1.2).

Natural Earth 5.1.2 admin-1 states and provinces (1:50m): the Crimea (UA-43) and Sevastopol
(UA-40) polygons, extracted unchanged into ne_50m_admin_1_crimea_sevastopol.geojson and used to
show them as part of Ukraine, as on UN maps (UN General Assembly resolution 68/262). Public domain.
Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_50m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson
(SHA-256 69a0e06e640b2d505858ae1cb63034e4677f3000b35a98e16312932b98c426b9; the extract's SHA-256 is
78984719dd109caa4191b0308edac92dd8aed63a5d13309e982e1da4e216a76b). The method is in
src/GlobeCore.Earth/Assets/README.md.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cloud imagery: NASA GIBS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Clouds: NASA GIBS (VIIRS NOAA-21 true colour). The daily image is downloaded at run time
(GlobeCore.Earth.Clouds.GibsCloudsFeed); it is not included. The test sample
tests/Samples/gibs_viirs_noaa21_truecolor_4096.jpg is one such image (2026-11-10).
